Martian Surface Radiation Model

This model provides radiation dose on Martian surface under different conditions: Solar modulation potential Φ and Mars-Sun angle (Solar longitude) Ls. You can input time and click to get the corresponding Φ and Ls, or directly input Φ and Ls.
Recommended browsers: Chrome 86+ or Edge 86+
